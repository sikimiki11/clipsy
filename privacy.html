Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Clipsy ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads videos to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What we collect

  • Basic profile info (such as your display name and user ID) from TikTok or Instagram, to know which account to post to.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok or Instagram when you log in, stored by our integration provider (Composio) to make posts on your behalf.
  • The videos and captions you choose to upload.

How we use it

Only to upload and publish the videos you select. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok or Instagram settings, or ask us to delete your data via the contact below.

Third parties

TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and Composio process data under their own privacy policies.
